Converts Retention Platform

A church home for every new decision.

When people respond to the gospel at a crusade, conference or mass event, 1016 sends each new convert to the nearest church, then walks with them through four levels of growth.

Pilot locations: Lagos and Abuja

John 10:16
How it works

From the altar call to a named church.

  1. Decide

    A person responds at an event and fills in one short form.

  2. Matched

    The form becomes a profile. It reaches the nearest partner church before the convert signs up.

  3. Welcomed

    The church follows up. The convert signs up to join membership activities, and signs in on return visits.

  4. Growing

    Courses lead through four maturity levels, and the library stays open even to people who do not attend that church.

Who it serves

Built around three people.

New converts

Found, not lost.

  • A find-a-church map shows the nearest partner church.
  • One form at the event. No repeated paperwork.
  • A USSD and SMS channel is planned for people without smartphones.
  • Free to the convert.
Churches

Your flock, in your name.

  • Join by QR code and run the app under your own branding.
  • An admin portal for membership management, to replace WhatsApp groups.
  • Your course outline shapes the maturity path for your members.
  • Data handled securely, with periodic reports to church headquarters.
Event organisers

Follow-up that stays neutral.

  • Converts are routed by location, with no steering toward any one church.
  • An organiser can recommend its own ministry as an opt-in choice in the portal.
  • Large annual and biannual events are the starting point.
Growth path

Foundations first, then weightier matters.

The path follows Hebrews 6:1. It begins with the foundations of the faith and rises through four levels. Each church can tailor it to its own course outline.

Level 1

Foundations

Repentance, faith toward God, baptisms and the first teachings.

Level 2

Rooted

Habits of prayer, the Word and belonging in a local church.

Level 3

Serving

Finding a place to serve and carry responsibility in the fold.

Level 4

Weightier matters

Deeper study for believers ready to go beyond the foundations.

Beyond the four levels, a topical library of books is planned.
